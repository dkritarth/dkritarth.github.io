# Graduate Research Assistant — Michigan State University

I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the Data Mining Laboratory at Michigan State University, advised by Prof. Pang-Ning Tan.

- I currently work on spatiotemporal machine learning and deep learning-based weather forecasting for complex environmental systems.
- I build reproducible pipelines for scientific datasets, model training, experimentation, and evaluation.
- My upcoming research will focus on adversarial robustness and generalization in ensemble forecasting models under changing environmental conditions.
- Current interests: spatiotemporal ML, deep learning, time-series forecasting, graph neural networks, generative models, AI robustness, and scientific ML.
