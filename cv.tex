\section*{Professional Summary}
Computer Science PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant at Michigan State University, studying spatiotemporal machine learning, deep learning based weather forecasting, and adversarial robustness. My current research examines learned weather ensembles, their sensitivity to constrained input perturbations, and the evaluation of deterministic, probabilistic, and hybrid forecasting systems. I previously conducted research in digital health and computational materials science while completing an accelerated three-year bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University at Buffalo with a 3.8 GPA.
